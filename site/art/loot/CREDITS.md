# Painted loot artwork

Created for Velua with the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool on 2026-10-18.

- `accessories-v2.webp`: 35 accessory designs (5 slots × 7 themes).
- `supplies.webp`: six supplies, gold coins and essence crystals.
- `manifest.json`: exact source frames, dimensions and SHA-256 hashes.
- Original RGBA sources and generation/repair prompts: `artifacts/loot-art-v2/source/` in the project.
- Compression and frame measurement: `scripts/pack-loot-art.py` (libwebp/cwebp).

Existing equipment artwork remains in `public/art/equipment/`. No external stock assets or license-bearing preset packs were added in this update.
